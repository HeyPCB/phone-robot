# Bolt, the phone stand robot

**Hook:** A little robot that holds your phone, charges it, and peeks over the top to tell you how it's eating.

**Description (60 words):**
Bolt holds your phone sideways for calls, recipes and shows, and charges it gently through its USB-C feet. Its
gold eyes peek over the top: orange while your phone gulps power, green when it's full. Snap its hands
out of its belly and slot them through its hips: no tools. Tap its antenna for night mode. Matte black,
real gold.

**Suggested price:** $29

1. A home-office desk at golden hour: Bolt holding a phone on a video call beside a laptop, its gold eyes
   glowing orange over the phone's top edge.
2. Close-up of the build: hands snapping the two gold-hearted kickstands out of Bolt's belly, then
   sliding one through its hip.
3. A kitchen counter: a recipe on the phone, flour on the counter, Bolt's eyes turned green because the
   phone is full.
